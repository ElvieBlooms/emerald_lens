EMERALD LENS SPRITE TOOLS: TRY THEM ON YOUR COMPUTER
=====================================================

The sprite tools turn a sprite sheet into a character folder for Emerald
Lens. They run in your web browser, and your art stays on your computer:
nothing is uploaded anywhere.

You need: a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, and an internet
connection the first time (the page downloads about 13 MB of Python).


1. GET THE FILES
----------------
Download emerald_lens_tools-<version>.zip from the latest release at
https://github.com/ElvieBlooms/emerald_lens/releases and unzip it. The
files below are in the folder it makes. (They're also in the "tools"
folder of the repo, if you download the whole thing with GitHub's green
"Code" button.)


2. START IT
-----------
WINDOWS
  Double-click "Start sprite tools (Windows).bat".
  If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then
  "Run anyway".

MAC
  Double-click "Start sprite tools (Mac).command".
  The first time, macOS may say it can't be opened. Right-click (or
  Control-click) the file, choose "Open", then "Open" again.
  If it says you don't have permission: open the Terminal app, type
  sh  (with a space after it), drag the file into the Terminal window,
  and press Return.

LINUX
  Open a terminal in that folder and run:
      sh start-sprite-tools.sh

A small window opens and your browser shows the tools. If the browser
doesn't open by itself, copy the address shown in the window into it.

Leave that window open while you use the tools. Close it when you're done.


3. TELL US WHAT BROKE
---------------------
Anything odd is worth reporting: an error, a button that does nothing, a
sprite in the wrong place, something confusing. Please include:

  - what you clicked or did, step by step;
  - what happened, and what you expected;
  - the grey log text under the step (select it and copy it);
  - your system (Windows, Mac, Linux) and browser;
  - the sheet you used, if you're allowed to share it.

Post it in the Emerald Lens thread on the gen1recomp Discord, or open an
issue at https://github.com/ElvieBlooms/emerald_lens/issues

Please don't share sprites ripped from the games, and credit the artist of
any fan art you post.
